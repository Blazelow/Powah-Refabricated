**Powah! Refabricated v6.2.10**

A Fabric port of Powah! for Minecraft 1.21.1, based on the NeoForge version 6.2.10.

&nbsp;

**What's included:**

- Generators: Furnator, Magmator, Thermo Generator, Solar Panel and Reactor
- Energy Cells, Energy Cables, Ender Cells and Ender Gates
- Energizing Orb and Rods, Energy Hopper, Energy Discharger and Player Transmitter
- Batteries, Capacitors, Wrench, Binding Cards and the other items, plus Uraninite ore and Dry Ice
- Energy works through Team Reborn Energy, so Powah machines connect to other Fabric energy mods
- Optional Trinkets support: batteries in trinket slots are charged
- The in-game Powah guide book

&nbsp;

**Differences from the NeoForge version:**

- The guide book is a simpler built-in book, because GuideME is not available on Fabric. Energy values shown in it are the default config values
- Curios support is replaced by Trinkets support
- Requires Fabric API and Cloth Config
